Barrelmaw Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Barrelmaw is a pirate puzzle-card game. This policy explains what the game stores and what it does not.

What the game stores

Your progress (cleared stages, stars, cards, chests, Doubloons, Pearls, sound setting and the daily-crate date) is saved on your own device, in the browser's or app's local storage. The game does not send this data to us.

What we do not collect

The web version has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Playing inside TikTok

If you play Barrelmaw as a TikTok Mini Game, TikTok Login signs you in. TikTok gives the game an identifier that is specific to this game (the open ID) and the basic profile permission you approve. We use it only to save and restore your game progress. We do not use it for advertising and do not share it with other parties. TikTok's own handling of your data is described in TikTok's privacy policy.

Children

Barrelmaw is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Clearing the site data or uninstalling removes the progress saved on your device. If your progress is stored with us through TikTok Login, ask us to delete it using the contact below and we will remove it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/ihsanmahendra/barrelmaw/issues.
