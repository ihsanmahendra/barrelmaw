# Audio credits

- Sound effects `click`, `card`, `deal`, `sparkle`, `miss`, `win`, `lose` and `reward` come from the
  SoundWeaver pack (MIT license, copyright 2026 SoundWeaver contributors), normalized to a common level:
  click-crisp-primary, select-item, swipe, xp-up, invalid-thunk, achievement, error-soft-thud, coin-collect.
- `fire`, `hit`, `big`, `boom`, `cannon` and the two `bgm_*` music loops are synthesized by `tools/make_sfx.py`
  and `tools/make_bgm.py`. The original synthesized versions of the replaced sounds are in `synth_backup/`.
- Card pack sounds (`chest_shake`, `chest_open`, `flip`, `sting_common`, `sting_rare`, `sting_epic`,
  `sting_legendary`, `sting_transcended`, `merge`, `pearl`) are synthesized by `tools/make_sfx.py`.
