## Description (paste as is)

Barrelmaw is a pirate puzzle-card game. Aim your cannon at stacks of colored barrels, plan each shot with the queue of cannonballs, and clear the board before you run out of turns. Play cards for special tricks, open chests to find new cards, and merge spare copies to level them up. Climb 100 stages from sunny beaches to night seas, beat the bosses and earn three stars on every stage. Quick to learn, easy to play with one hand.

## Things to know before submitting

1. This form registers the app. The Godot web build cannot be uploaded as the game: TikTok accepts only Cocos Creator 3.x, Unity 2021-2022 or Laya 3.x
   packages (see `tiktok-mini-games-plan.md`).
2. `github.io` is a domain shared by everyone, so TikTok may not let you verify it as yours. If the form or review rejects the domain, buy a cheap domain,
   point it at GitHub Pages and give the Terms and Privacy URLs on that domain.
3. The privacy policy covers TikTok Login (open ID used to save progress). If you later add ads, analytics or purchases, update both pages first.
4. The contact on both pages is the GitHub issues page of the repo. Replace it with an email if TikTok asks for one.
